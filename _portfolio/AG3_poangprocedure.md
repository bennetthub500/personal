## Ikea Poang assembly documentation exercise


### Workspace prerequisites

To assemble the Poang chair you will need:

- A level floorspace
- A soft floor mat


### Equipment prerequisites

All the tools and fasteners you will need are provided:
- 1 Screwdriver 
- 1 Allen wrench 
- 4 long threaded Allen fasteners, numbered 12206 or 12204
- 2 medium threaded Allen fasteners, numbered 146654 or 146655
- 2 support pins, numbered 123786
- 8 small threaded Allen fasteners, numbered 119046 or 123257
- 6 small threaded Allen fasteners, numbered 120925 or 150450
- 4 slip-in bolt anchors, numbered 100514
- 2 rubber washers, numbered 102319

**Note**: power drivers are not recommended, as they can strip fasteners if not used carefully.


### Assembly steps

1.  Locate the six matching equally-sized curved wood chair pieces. These are horizontal ribs for the chair’s back. In the first step you’ll be using 4 of them. 

2. Locate the two long matching curved pieces that are longer than the rib pieces in the previous step.  These are the chair back vertical side pieces.  The large pieces needed for these first steps are not the very large C-shaped side pieces. 

3. Place one of the chair back vertical side pieces on the floor mat, with the open side slots facing up.  Note the screw holes on one side - this is the front of the chair back; when seated your back will be against this side with the holes. 

4. Fit the four rib pieces, one at a time, into the slots on the chair back vertical side piece. 
      - The orientation for all of the rib pieces should have the convex-side against your back when seated.  
      - One of the rib pieces has velcro on its convex side. This rib must be fitted in the second position from the top of the chair back vertical side piece. The top end of the chair back vertical side piece does not have a protruding tab with a hole.  
      - Once fitted, all rib pieces will remain standing up, like an ‘E’ lying on its side.

Tip: To help you visualize the completed chair, look at the convex-side-up pieces and the orientation of the other chair pieces, and try to imagine seated surfaces that have a gentle curve that will hug your body. 

5. Next fit the second chair back vertical side piece on top of the ribs that are standing up and already fitted into the other side piece. 

6. Find the bag of Allen fasteners marked 119046 or 123257. 

7. Screw four of the 119046 or 12357 fasteners into the holes on one of the chair back side pieces, and then do the same for the second chair back side piece. 

Your chair back frame is now completed. 

8. Find the partially pre-assembled seat section with the fabric attached. 

9. Find the 120925 or 150450 fasteners.

10. Fit the curved ends of the pre-assembled seat section into the bottom ends of the chair back frame so that the holes in the tabs are aligned. 

11. Fasten these two sections together with the 120925 or 150450 fasteners

You now have the chair back attached to the chair bottom. 

12. Locate the remaining two rib pieces. 

13. Slide the ribs from the side of the chair into the sleeves in the fabric bottom, convex-side up. 

14. Fasten the newly-inserted ribs to the chair bottom with the four remaining 120925 or 150450 fasteners.

15. Locate the two large C-shaped side pieces.

Tip: These two C-shaped pieces will fit on each side of the chair back and bottom sections you assembled.  Once completed, the flat part of the C-shaped piece will sit on the floor, and the angled-end of the C-shaped piece will attach to the chair back.

16. Locate the 146654 or 146655 Allen fasteners and the 102399 washers.

17. Place the chair back and bottom you assembled on the floor, resting on one of its side edges. 

18. Locate the 2 support pins, numbered 123786.

19. Insert one of the 123786 pins in the hole on the outside edge of the seat bottom, approximately 3 inches from the end. 

20. Using the 146654 or 146655 Allen fasteners, attach the angled-end of the C-shaped piece to the only screw hole on the outer side edge of the vertical side bar of the chair back.   Place a washer between the C-shaped piece and the side bar on each side.

Nex, you will attach the remaining two crossbar pieces to the C-shaped piece.

21. Locate the long fasteners numbered 122606 or 122604 and the 4 slip-in bolt anchors, numbered 100514.

22. Fit one of the crossbars to the flat end of the C-shaped piece.  Note: the screw holes on the flat side of the crossbar should face down, towards the floor.  It should not be visible from above once the chair is completed. 

23. Place a slip-in bolt anchor into the screw hole.

24. Screw in one of the large 122606 or 122604 fasteners, aligning the fastener with the threaded hole in the slip-in bolt anchor.  

25. Fit the other crossbar to the front of the C-shaped piece under the seat bottom, in the center of the C.  

26. As with the other crossbar, Screw in one of the large 122606 or 122604 fasteners, aligning the fastener with the threaded hole in the slip-in bolt anchor.  

27. Carefully flip the chair over onto its other side.  The C-shaped piece will now be against the floor mat. 

28. Fit the other C-shaped piece into place as you completed it on the other side. 

29. Using the 146654 or 146655 Allen fasteners, attach the angled-end of the C-shaped piece to the only screw hole on the outer side edge of the vertical side bar of the chair back.  Place a washer between the C-shaped piece and the side bar on each side.

30. Attach the C-shaped piece to the rear crossbar: Screw in one of the large 122606 or 122604 fasteners, aligning the fastener with the threaded hole in the slip-in bolt anchor.  

31. Fit the C-shaped piece to the front cross bar, under the seat bottom, in the center of the C. 

32. Screw in one of the large 122606 or 122604 fasteners, aligning the fastener with the threaded hole in the slip-in bolt anchor.  

You’re now ready to complete the assembly by placing the padded cushion on the chair. 


### Troubleshooting

**The screws don’t go all the way in**  
You may have used the incorrect screw for the relevant step.  This probably means a second screw somewhere else did not fit.  Check the screw size and number in the  instructions to ensure you are using the right screw. 

**The screws don’t seem to be holding**
You may have forgotten to use the slip-in bolt anchors, numbered 100514.  Return to the instructions for that piece and make sure the slip-in anchors are utilized. 

**The chair does not feel right on my legs or back**
You may have not installed one of the ribs in the correct convex orientation in either the chair back or seat.  If this is the case, you will have to disassemble the chair and repeat the process. Check to make sure all of the ribs are curved in the same orientation, as if your body would fit into a C-shaped bend in all the ribs. 

**The seat cushion slides off the back**
You may not have installed the rib with the velcro piece in the second position on the chair back.  Check the instructions to make sure it is properly installed.
